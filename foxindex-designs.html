Three steps from publish to indexed

Connect your site once. After that, indexing a page is a paste and a click.

Step 1

Connect your site

Add your domain, drop one small key file on it for IndexNow, and connect Search Console with Google sign-in. Takes about two minutes.

Step 2

Submit URLs

Paste a list, upload a CSV, or point us at your sitemap. Each URL is pushed to every engine you've enabled, in order of priority.

Step 3

Watch them get indexed

We re-check each URL against Google every few hours and resubmit anything that hasn't landed. You see the exact timestamp it went live.

Questions people ask before signing up

Why does my account need approval?

Every account is checked by a real manager before it goes live. It keeps spam sites off the platform, which keeps our submission quotas healthy for everyone. Most approvals happen within a few hours.

Does one credit mean one URL?

One credit covers one URL submitted to Google. IndexNow engines (Bing, Yandex and others) are included free with every submission.

What if a page never gets indexed?

We resubmit it up to three times through different routes. If Google still declines it, the credit is refunded automatically and the URL is flagged so you can check the page itself.

Can I add credit without a card?

Yes. Your account manager can add credit manually after a bank transfer, bKash, or any method you agree on.
